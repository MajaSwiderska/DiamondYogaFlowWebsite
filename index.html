About the Instructor

Hi everyone, my name is Aga (Agnieszka). I am the founder of a women-owned community wellness studio that focuses on yoga, sound therapy, and nervous system regulation.

The studio was created in response to the growing levels of stress, burnout, and mental overload experienced by working adults — especially women balancing work, family, and daily responsibilities.

My work combines mindful movement with therapeutic sound practices to support mental health, reduce stress, and help clients reconnect with their bodies in a calm, safe, and supportive environment. The studio welcomes people of all levels and backgrounds, including beginners who may feel intimidated by traditional fitness spaces.
